The lead

An engineer who flies to where the model actually has to work.

Kiran Machha is a Forward Deployed AI Engineer. He sits inside the customer's team — their repo, their cluster, their compliance review — and takes an AI system from a promising notebook to something on-call can defend at three in the morning. Enterprise delivery across banking, payments, telecom, logistics, ecommerce and government, based in Singapore.

The specialism is sovereign AI — open-weight models running inside the customer's own datacentre, VPC or air-gapped network. Banks, hospitals and government suppliers who want the capability but cannot send a single record to a third-party API. Everything in the stack is open source and self-hosted, so nothing is rented and no vendor holds the keys.

That covers the whole line: tuning open models on the customer's own corpus, serving them on Kubernetes and GPUs at a cost per request that survives scrutiny, observing them with self-hosted tracing and evals, and integrating them with the ERP, the core banking API and the ticketing queue they already run on.

Most of it is published in the open — LLMs on Kubernetes, KubeRay and GPU quickstarts, agent harnesses, RAG platforms for regulated industries — as workshops other engineers can run end to end.

Domains · where the work has landed

Every industry has its own definition of “done”.

The model is the easy part. What changes between sectors is the data, the regulator, the tolerance for a wrong answer, and who has to sign the release.

Banking

Regulated by default

Document-heavy processes with an auditor at the end of them. Nothing ships without a trail showing what the system saw and why it acted.

Cheque & invoice OCR · Corporate RAG · Evidence logs · Human approval gates
Payments

Latency and reconciliation

High-volume flows where a duplicate is worse than a delay, and a wrongly held transaction is a customer lost.

Fraud scoring & alert triage · Card disputes · Reconciliation · Idempotent retries
Telecom

Volume and voice

Enormous ticket and call volumes where a few points of deflection is real money, and the NOC never stops.

STT / TTS care agents · Alarm correlation · Runbook automation · Knowledge retrieval
Logistics

Documents in motion

Paperwork that travels with the freight and arrives as photographs, plus disputes that hinge on what a pallet looked like on arrival.

Bill-of-lading & customs OCR · Dock damage vision · Exception routing · Edge inference
Ecommerce

Intent, at speed

Consumer-facing AI judged on responsiveness and taste, where every extra hundred milliseconds is measurable in conversion.

Semantic product discovery · Recommendations · Returns & refunds · Support deflection
Government & healthcare

Sovereignty first

Estates where the data legally cannot leave, autonomy is granted last, and the audit chain has to belong to the institution.

On-prem & air-gapped inference · PII redaction · Citizen & clinical retrieval · Owned audit trail

Banking & logistics · Document AI

Invoice Processing OCR

ProblemInvoices arrive as scans, email attachments and phone photographs, then get re-keyed by hand into an ERP with dozens of required fields and no duplicate check.
AgentsExtractor reads the document and line items · Matcher reconciles against the purchase order and contract · Poster drafts the ERP record and waits for approval.
ControlsField-level confidence thresholds, duplicate detection across the vendor history, and a human approval gate before anything posts. Every run keeps the image crop it read from.
PaddleOCR · docTR · Qwen2-VL · LayoutLMv3 · LangGraph · vLLM · PGVector · FastAPI · MinIO · Langfuse
Banking · Document AI

Cheque OCR & Clearing

ProblemClearing depends on reading handwriting — courtesy amount, legal amount, payee and the MICR band — under a regime where one wrong digit is a real loss.
AgentsReader recognises both amount fields independently · Cross-checker compares them against each other and the MICR line · Referrer escalates any disagreement rather than resolving it.
ControlsDisagreement between the two amount reads is an automatic referral. Decisions are logged with the crop that produced them so an auditor can replay the call.
TrOCR · CRNN · OpenCV · MICR parsing · ONNX Runtime · Triton · PostgreSQL · Evidently · Grafana
Banking & ecommerce · Agentic

Card Dispute & Refund Agent

ProblemChargebacks and refund claims arrive faster than analysts can work them, and each one needs scheme rules, the transaction trail and the merchant's evidence pulled together before a deadline.
AgentsIntake classifies the reason code · Evidence gathers the transaction, delivery and comms trail through typed tools · Drafter writes the representment against the scheme rulebook.
ControlsAn LLM-as-judge scores whether the evidence actually supports the claim before a human ever sees it; weak packs go back for more. Money never moves without a person.
LangGraph · MCP tools · Qdrant · bge-reranker · Phoenix LLM-as-judge · LiteLLM gateway · NeMo Guardrails · Keycloak
Payments · Risk

Fraud Detection & Alert Triage

ProblemClassical scoring flags far more than the team can review, so genuine fraud waits in a queue behind false positives nobody has had time to close.
AgentsScorer — a gradient-boosted and graph model, not an LLM · Triage assembles the case narrative and prior behaviour · Responder proposes hold, step-up verification or release.
ControlsThe model decides risk; the LLM only explains and assembles. Drift and score distribution are monitored continuously, and every autonomous hold is reversible.
Kafka · XGBoost · PyTorch Geometric · Feast · Redis · LangGraph · Evidently · Prometheus · Grafana
Finance operations · Agentic

Accounting Close & Reconciliation

ProblemMonth-end runs on spreadsheets and institutional memory: matching ledgers, chasing unexplained variances and writing the same commentary every period.
AgentsReconciler matches ledger and bank lines · Investigator traces unmatched items back through source systems · Narrator drafts the variance commentary for the controller.
ControlsEvery figure in the narrative is traceable to a journal line — the agent may not state a number it cannot cite. The controller signs; the agent never posts.
LangGraph · Instructor / Pydantic schemas · PostgreSQL · ERP APIs · Airflow · vLLM · RAGAS · Langfuse
Enterprise & government · RAG

Corporate RAG Knowledge System

ProblemPolicies, contracts and SOPs spread across SharePoint, Confluence and shared drives, where the answer exists but nobody can tell which version is current or who is cleared to read it.
AgentsPlanner decomposes the question · Retriever runs hybrid search with re-ranking · Answerer composes with enforced citations · Judge grades the answer before release.
ControlsPermission-aware retrieval at query time, not filtering afterwards. An answer without a source is refused, and a golden dataset catches regressions before a release ships.
LangGraph · BM25 + PGVector hybrid · bge-reranker · Qdrant · RAGAS · DeepEval · Keycloak RBAC · Langfuse

Telecom · Operations

Network Operations Assistant

ProblemA NOC drowning in alarms, where the same incident is diagnosed from scratch each shift because the runbook lives in three wikis and one engineer's head.
AgentsCorrelator groups alarms into one incident · Diagnostician queries metrics, logs and topology through typed tools · Runbook proposes the remediation step and the rollback.
ControlsRead-only by default. Any command that changes network state is proposed, not executed, until an engineer approves — and every proposal cites the metric that justified it.

AI Guardrails & Observability

ProblemOnce agents can act, the risk stops being a bad sentence and becomes a bad action — and nobody can say whether quality improved last week or what any of it cost.

ControlsPrompt-injection and jailbreak suites run in CI, PII is redacted before it reaches a model, and tool calls are policy-checked — self-hosted throughout, so telemetry never leaves the estate.
